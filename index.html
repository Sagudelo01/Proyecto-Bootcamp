Methodology & sources

Figures come from open datasets published by Our World in Data: the share of electricity from renewables, installed solar PV capacity and installed geothermal capacity. The raw CSVs are filtered to a set of key regions and pre-processed into a small JavaScript bundle, so the dashboard loads instantly with no backend. Our World in Data.

Limitations

  • The dataset is trimmed to 26 regions and countries, not the full world.
  • Values end around 2021 and some regions miss certain series.
  • Shares are of electricity generation, not total final energy.
